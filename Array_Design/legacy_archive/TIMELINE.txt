TIMELINE -- Array_Design/legacy_archive/
Simple dated log of what was tried and abandoned. Full technical detail for every
entry below is in this same folder's README.md -- this file is just the short
date+reason version.

All 7 defects below were found and archived on the SAME audit day:

2026-08-16 -- grid_N=2048 wave-optics propagation grid -- ABANDONED, superseded by
  grid_N=4096 -- reason: gave an aperture-independent artifact value (identical blur
  to 11 decimals for different aperture sizes); confirmed 2x-too-large vs the 4096
  rerun. See 01_gridN2048_artifact/.

2026-08-16 -- ghost-separation formula "delta_r = s*v_r/d_gap" -- ABANDONED,
  superseded by "delta_r = s*M/(P0-M)" -- reason: does not vanish at M=0 (geometrically
  required to); R²=-1701.7 against real propagation data (worse than predicting the
  mean), vs R²=0.995 for the replacement. See 02_legacy_ghost_formula/.

2026-08-16 -- alignment-offset ensemble at n_offsets=15 -- ABANDONED, superseded by
  n_offsets=61 -- reason: too coarse to resolve worst-case alignment reliably (uses
  the already-correct ghost formula, just under-sampled). See
  03_insufficient_alignment_sampling/.

2026-08-16 -- fixed straight-ahead-gaze-only (phi=0) safety check -- ABANDONED,
  superseded by full gaze-angle integration -- reason: 37.8%-88.9% of phi=0-safe
  designs (family-dependent) fail once gaze rotation is checked. Also in this
  folder: a placeholder UNIFORM gaze-usage weighting -- ABANDONED, superseded by a
  literature-grounded Gaussian (sigma ~6 deg, from measured saccade-amplitude
  statistics) -- though the field-averaged robustness CONCLUSION held under both.
  See 04_pre_vignetting/.

2026-08-16 -- sweep development snapshots legacy1-legacy6 -- ABANDONED, superseded by
  later stabilized runs -- reason: ordinary iteration while the family set/seed
  handling/column schema were still being finalized, not a specific physics defect.
  One plot from this era was entirely blank (max()-aggregation bug, fixed by
  switching to median). See 05_superseded_sweep_iterations/.

2026-08-16 -- packing-density metric (pre-cleanup) -- ABANDONED, superseded by a
  Minkowski/offset-buffer-corrected version -- reason: 412/6615 rows (6.2%) reported
  open-area fraction > 1.0 (physically impossible); the M=0 and global winners under
  this metric were a single stray hole (N_actual=1) misranked as the densest design
  in the study. See 06_degeneracy_contaminated/.

2026-08-16 -- lattice sweep at a single fixed orientation (no rotation parameter) --
  ABANDONED, superseded by a 0/15/30/45-degree rotation sweep -- reason: rotation
  changed metrics in 76% of 810 tested parameter groups (mostly via which holes
  survive overlap rejection) -- not a no-op, so the un-rotated sweep is an
  incomplete design space, not a wrong-but-usable one. See 07_pre_rotation/.

2026-09-13 -- entire candidate pool/ranking chain (sweep_results.csv through
  gaze_weighted_winner_per_M.csv, FINAL_WINNER_PER_M.csv, shen_model outputs) --
  ABANDONED, superseded by a full pipeline re-run -- reason: a_hole(M) was sourced
  live from new_sweeps/per_M_optimum_map.csv, which was itself computed on a stale
  (grid_N=2048) acuity sweep (see new_sweeps/legacy_archive/
  01_acuity_gridN2048_artifact/). VERIFIED, not assumed: re-running the whole chain
  on the corrected acuity data produced byte-identical gaze_weighted_winner_per_M.csv
  and FINAL_WINNER_PER_M.csv (and byte-identical fabrication_package masks) -- the
  bug never actually changed which aperture won, only the intermediate pool's raw
  numbers. See 08_stale_acuity_aperture_artifact/.
