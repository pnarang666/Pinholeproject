2026-08-14 (Addendum, "second pass") -- scored-optimum work -- includes a real bug
  found and fixed while building the multi-hole blur sweep: array_psf_envelope.py
  measured blur as distance from the OPTICAL AXIS instead of from the summed
  pattern's own intensity centroid, which for a single off-center active hole (N_eff=1)
  reported r_EE_50 growing from 11um to 86um purely from being off-axis, with zero
  actual change in image sharpness. Found by tracing an unexpected optimizer result
  back to its source, not by a dedicated test. FIXED by recentering on the summed
  pattern's own centroid; the fix changed individual alignment values on re-run but
  not the optimizer's chosen (aperture, pitch) at any (M, packing) -- diluted by
  averaging in the reported stats, though it would matter to anyone reading
  array_r_EE_50_min/max_um directly.
