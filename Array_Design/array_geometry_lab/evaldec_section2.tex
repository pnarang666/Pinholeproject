\section{Findings Per Model}

\subsection{Model A --- Worst-Case Gates}
\textbf{Strengths.} Simple, conservative in eccentricity, produces hard
pass/fail guarantees. Correctly rejected every genuinely broken design
found during this project's audits (blackout candidates, boundary
near-misses, seed-lottery winners).

\textbf{Defects found.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Single-azimuth sampling} (Section 2) --- misses blind
  spots in 19.8\% of candidates. This is the most serious defect in
  either model.
  \item \textbf{Density objective reduces to $1/\text{hull area}$} when
  only one hole is active (true for M=2--8), so it effectively minimises
  array size rather than maximising light. It also scales with
  $N_{\rm requested}$, systematically favouring the smallest tested $N$
  --- every Model A winner sits at the minimum $N$ swept.
  \item \textbf{Unweighted eccentricity} --- treats 25$^\circ$ (0.58\% of
  viewing time) as heavily as 0$^\circ$ (9.29\%), disqualifying designs
  on failures the wearer would essentially never encounter.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Model B --- Gaze-Weighted}
\textbf{Strengths.} Azimuthally complete. Weights by measured viewing-time
distribution (verified: weights sum to 100.0000, cumulative 51.61\% by
6$^\circ$ and 95.27\% by 17$^\circ$, all three assertions passing).
Outputs a directly interpretable quantity (``\% of viewing time
ghosting'') rather than an abstract margin.

\textbf{Defects and limitations.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Large tied plateaus} --- many designs reach exactly
  0.00\%/0.00\%, so the reported ``winner'' is one representative of a
  tie chosen by insertion order, not a uniquely optimal design. The
  comparison report states this explicitly rather than implying a
  sharper distinction than the data supports.
  \item \textbf{No density or light-throughput term} --- it optimises
  purely for safety, so among equally-safe designs it has no basis to
  prefer the one delivering more light.
  \item \textbf{Depends on one empirical distribution} --- a different
  gaze dataset would shift the weights, though the sharp central peak is
  robust across the literature.
\end{itemize}
