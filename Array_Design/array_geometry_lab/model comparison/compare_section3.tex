\section{Worked Example: M=2.0 Diopters}

With $\mu_g$ neutralized, Shen's score collapses to fill-factor closeness
($\mu_\eta$) and the fixed geometric blind-rate constant per family --- \emph{not}
to anything reflecting whether a design actually ghosts.

The hexagonal candidate wins Shen's score by sitting closer to the target
fill factor and carrying a lower structural blind-rate floor --- while its
actual, independently computed ghost and vignetting margins are strongly
negative. The published formula never registers this, because its one
safety-related term is saturated to zero for every candidate in this
regime, including this one.

\section{Root Causes of the Divergence}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Scale mismatch in the ghost term.} $g=(sf'/L)/c_{\text{tot}}$
  compares hole spacing ($s\sim1$--4\,mm in this project) against blur
  ($c_{\text{tot}}\sim3$--17\,$\mu$m). At that four-orders-of-magnitude
  ratio, the sigmoid is saturated by construction --- Shen's own text
  acknowledges this term ``exceeds unity by one to two orders of magnitude
  for feasible designs'' and ``cannot discriminate between designs''; this
  project's data shows the saturation is total, not partial.
  \item \textbf{Hard gates vs.\ soft membership.} A design with strongly
  negative real margin (e.g.\ $-13.8$) is eliminated outright by this
  project's gates. Under a soft membership function that never reaches
  exactly zero, no amount of real ghosting risk can be fully penalized ---
  it can only be outweighed by other terms.
  \item \textbf{No vignetting term in Shen's score.} $a_{\text{norm}}$,
  $\mu_\eta$, $\mu_g$ and coverage never reference gaze-angle-dependent
  safety. This project established earlier that straight-ahead ghost
  safety fails to predict gaze-integrated safety for 37.8\%--88.9\% of
  designs, depending on family --- an entire failure mode invisible to the
  published score.
\end{enumerate}
