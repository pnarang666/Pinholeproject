\section{Conclusion}

The divergence between this project's ranking and Shen's, on this
project's own candidate set, traces to one specific, verifiable mechanism:
Shen's ghost-safety term is a smooth membership function calibrated for a
different scale relationship between hole spacing and blur than this
project's devices exhibit, and it saturates to numerically zero across
100\% of tested candidates as a result. This is not evidence that Shen's
published lattice ranking is wrong in its own tested regime --- their
evaluator normalizes every design to a fixed fill factor and covers a
broader shape and arrangement space than this project has swept. It is
evidence that applying Shen's formula literally to this project's specific
candidates produces a ranking decided almost entirely by fill-factor
closeness and a fixed per-family geometric constant, not by ghosting risk,
because the formula's own safety term never activates in this regime.

This project's hard-gated pipeline, using the same real, pairwise-computed
margin at every ranking step, does not share this failure mode --- and
where its own analogous defects have been found (boundary-adjacent
``winners'', a density formula biased across families), they have been
identified, fixed, and re-verified against independent hand calculation.

\textbf{Recommendation:} retain this project's hard-gated pipeline as the
primary ranking system. Adopt Shen's closed-form blind-rate floors as a
supplementary reported metric. Cite Shen (2026) as prior art addressing the
same problem, with this document's Sections 3--4 as the basis for a
specific, narrow technical differentiation --- not a claim of general
superiority.
