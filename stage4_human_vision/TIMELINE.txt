- "Pass 2" (dated 2026-08-06 per CHANGES.md's own framing, triggered
  directly by the Pass-1 validation failure above):
    1. acuity.py predict_acuity() -- replaced an uncited sqrt(snr)/10
       exponent term with a cited additive logMAR correction (Tidbury,
       Czanner & Newsham 2016 for the slope; Van Nes & Bouman 1967 for
       the 300 td knee). The OLD term is KEPT, unchanged, as
       vsotf_snr_legacy in the returned models dict -- not deleted, both
       remain available.
    2. calibration.py (new) -- fit a linear calibration against Wang 2009
       with leave-one-out cross-validation. In-sample R^2 looked
       plausible (0.13-0.16), but leave-one-out R^2 was NEGATIVE in every
       case (-0.54 to -0.59) and Pearson r flipped sign between in-sample
       and held-out fits -- strong evidence the in-sample fit was noise.
       STATUS: this calibration attempt is an ABANDONED/FAILED approach,
       reported honestly per the project's own stated brief ("fit once,
       reported honestly, NOT tuned further to force a better number").
    3. An external check against a second real dataset (Kim et al. 2017,
       n=36) also missed: predicted 0.499 logMAR vs observed 0.20+/-0.21
       -- outside 1 standard deviation.
    4. outputs/stage4_full_sweep.csv and validation CSVs/PNGs
       regenerated; logmar_recommended and snellen_denominator updated in
       place, logmar_vsotf_snr_legacy and logmar_calibrated added as new
       columns (old mtf10/mtf50 columns untouched).

- HONEST CONCLUSION (stated directly in this folder's own RESULTS.md,
  not softened here): "this cannot be properly validated with the
  clinical data currently available publicly." Root causes diagnosed:
  n=5 is too small; the Wang dataset itself is non-monotonic
  (0.28->0.31->1.00->0.40->0.50 across 5 apertures, an unexplained spike
  no smooth model can fit); no available dataset has per-subject
  refractive error.

STATUS (current): the CLINICAL-ACUITY-VALIDATION goal this stage was
