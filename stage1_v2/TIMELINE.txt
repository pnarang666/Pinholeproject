  to fix a real bug: FWHM/1/e^2/custom-threshold radius jumping tens of

  fwhm_radius() reporting the FIRST radial sample below half-max, which

  adds sub-bin parabolic interpolation (removing a staircase artifact),
  and corrects the reference "peak" measurement, which had been ~2.5x low
  from averaging over the first annulus instead of resolving the on-axis
  spike.
