\section*{Methodology: how PASS/FAIL is determined}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Methodology}
Every row is one (hole diameter $N$, pitch $s$, packing) design, evaluated across every
tested myopia level $M$ and reported at its \textbf{worst case across $M$}. Two independent
conditions must both hold for a design to be \texttt{Safe}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Ghosting risk} $<1.0$ -- the continuous risk model
($P(\Neff\geq2)\times$worst-harmonic perceptual margin, Barten-1999-CSF-based), not the
deprecated binary Regime A/B/C gate. Risk $=1.0$ is the point where the probability-weighted
ghost comb crosses the eye's own contrast-detection threshold.
\item \textbf{Pitch margin} $\geq1.0$ -- pitch is at or beyond its own no-ghost geometric
ceiling ($s_{\text{solo}}$, the pitch above which a second hole can \emph{never} be
simultaneously active, for that packing). This is a structural (geometry-only) guarantee,
not a statistical one.
\end{enumerate}
A design that is \texttt{Safe} is then either \textbf{PASS -- Pareto-optimal}
(\texttt{recommended\_for\_prototyping=True}: on the Pareto front of blur vs.\ whole-lens
transmission, among safe designs) or \textbf{PASS -- dominated} (safe, but another safe
design is at least as good on both blur and transmission, and strictly better on one --
still physically safe, just not on the recommended frontier). A design that fails either
condition is \texttt{FAIL}, broken down by which condition(s) failed. \textbf{No numeric
transmission target (OD/\%T) is enforced anywhere} -- $T$ is reported for every row but is
not itself a pass/fail criterion (see this project's own \texttt{GAPS\_CLOSED.md} Gap 4).

\textbf{Column key}: $N$ = hole diameter ($\mu$m); $s$ = pitch ($\mu$m); $T$ = whole-lens
transmission (\%); blur = $r_{EE}(50\%)$ ($\mu$m, worst-case across $M$; historically named
\texttt{r\_EE\_80\_um} in the source CSV for schema reasons, see \texttt{decide.py}'s own
docstring); risk = worst-case-across-$M$ continuous ghosting risk; margin = pitch as a
fraction of its own no-ghost ceiling ($\geq1.0$ required to pass).
