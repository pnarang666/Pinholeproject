\section{Implementation Verification}
\label{sec:ch13-verification}

The \S13.1/\S13.2 formulas were implemented in \texttt{array\_optimization/vignetting.py}
as pure ray/aperture geometry, decoupled from the wave-optics propagator, and validated
against an independent Monte Carlo estimate (no shared code) -- converging to
$0.177$ percentage points of full hole area (tolerance $0.5$) after an initial,
statistically ill-conditioned tolerance metric (a naive relative-area ratio, which blows
up as true area $\to 0$ with no actual bias) was replaced with one normalized against
full hole area throughout. A boundary defect was also found and fixed: floating-point
roundoff at $\Delta = R_p{+}R_h$ produced a small negative area ($-0.059\,\mu\text{m}^2$);
the final combination is now clamped to $[0,\infty)$, and no negative values remain.

Applied to this project's 20 Pareto-optimal array candidates, gaze-angle field-averaging
over $\pm25^\circ$ (the validated range) does \textbf{not} change Pareto-front membership
for any candidate, under \emph{both} the uniform-placeholder and the
literature-grounded \texttt{gaussian\_centered} distribution (saccade-amplitude/center-bias
sourced; see \texttt{GAZE\_DISTRIBUTION\_SENSITIVITY.md} for citations). The two
distributions' field-averaged transmission estimates agree to within $0.02$ percentage
points -- a materially stronger robustness claim than one placeholder alone.

Secondary finding, held under both distributions: of the 13/20 candidates dark at primary
gaze ($N_{\text{eff,worst}}(\phi{=}0)=0$, ghosting-safe but momentarily lightless), 11--12
show measurably \emph{increased} $N_{\text{eff}}$ once realistic gaze movement is
integrated -- rotation can bring a hole into view, not only vignette one. Reported as a
real result, not smoothed into ``no change.''

Unresolved: $w(\phi)$ is grounded in saccade-\emph{amplitude}/center-bias literature, not
a direct measurement of gaze-\emph{dwell-time} while wearing pinhole sunglasses -- flagged
for future refinement, not presented as closed.
