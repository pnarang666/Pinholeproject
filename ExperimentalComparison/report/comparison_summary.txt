METHODOLOGY NOTES (found during this run, not assumed going in):
- sips writes a REAL 16-bit-per-channel TIFF (confirmed via TIFF tag
  inspection). The installed Pillow silently downcast this to 8-bit on
  array read -- caught before any measurement was finalized; all reading
  in this pipeline uses OpenCV instead, verified to preserve true uint16
  values losslessly. Exposure-flag thresholds are therefore 5%/95% of
  the native 0-65535 range, not literal 0/255.
- This machine had only ~1-3GB free disk system-wide during this run
  (pre-existing, unrelated to this task -- /Users volume was ~99% full).
  Full-resolution 16-bit TIFFs run ~290MB raw / ~176MB lossless-compressed
  each; 41 of them would not fit. Per your choice, tiff_converted/ holds a
  50%-linear-downsized, lossless-deflate-compressed copy of each image.
  ALL measurements (blob detection, centroid, r_EE_50) were computed on
  the FULL-RESOLUTION pixel data in memory before downsizing -- the kept
  TIFF files are a smaller copy for reference/viewing, not what was
  measured.
- Real phone-camera point-source photos in this set show a compact bright
  core plus a wide veiling-glare halo and thin near-peak diffraction
  spikes that connect to the frame edge or a secondary ghost blob at a
  naive low threshold. The blob-isolation threshold is auto-raised per
  image until the core bounding box is compact; see analyze.py docstring.

  21 measured/predicted ratio pairs available across all apertures.
  Mean |%deviation| = 24.3%, median = 12.8%, max = 79.3%.
  8/21 pairs diverge by more than 20% -- reported plainly above, not smoothed over.
  1200b has no exact match in blur_sweep.csv (nearest: 1000um, 1250um) --
  not interpolated or guessed; theory comparison is skipped for that folder.
  1000b's own M=0 file (010006.dng) does not match the folder's established
  filename pattern -- logged as unparseable, not guessed; 1000b's normalized
  ratios (both measured and predicted) are therefore unavailable.

  A clear pattern in the divergence itself, checked rather than assumed: for
  600b and 700b, measured blur growth plateaus (ratio stays roughly 1.0-1.1x)
  while theory predicts continued strong growth (up to ~3.3x for 600b, ~4.7x
  for 700b at M=8) -- deviation gets steadily WORSE at higher M for both
  apertures. Before reporting this as a real disagreement, two artifact
  explanations were checked and ruled out:
    (1) ROI/padding truncation -- verified directly on 600b/M=8: the found
        r_EE_50_px sits at only ~50% of the maximum radius available inside
        its analysis window, i.e. the algorithm had plenty of room to report
        a larger radius if the data supported one and did not hit a boundary.
    (2) Core pixel saturation -- plausible in principle (a clipped core could
        suppress the apparent growth of a photometric radius), but checked
        against the data and rejected as the sole explanation: 600b is
        saturated at EVERY M including M=0 (where deviation is 0% by
        definition), so saturation being present cannot by itself explain
        deviation getting worse specifically at higher M within that folder.
  With both of those ruled out, the plateau looks like a genuine measured
  effect, not a pipeline artifact -- but its actual physical cause (the real
  M-diopter lenses' optical power vs. nominal, sensor auto-exposure/ISP
  behavior at large defocus, or a genuine limitation of the theoretical
  model at large blur) is NOT determined by this data and is left as an open
  question rather than guessed.
