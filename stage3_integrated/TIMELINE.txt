  reference dataset, NOT an optimizer, and explicitly does not modify

  First step taken: cross-checked stage3a/transmission.py::

  combine_metrics.py then merged stage3_human_vision's 105-point metric
  sweep with stage3a's r_EE-percentile sweep into one 105-row, 32-column
  grid, with a hard-fail check if the two source grids ever produce a
  different row count.

  Three questions investigated directly (not assumed):
    1. Does a brightness floor explain the gap across ALL metrics, not
       just the original 4? YES, more strongly -- 8 of 10 tested metrics
       converge to exactly 1000um at every one of 7 myopia levels at a
       6.25% floor (extends stage3a's finding from 4 metrics to 10; only
       Strehl and throughput diverge, both already flagged unstable/
       trivial elsewhere).
    2. Is Strehl's non-monotonicity genuine or a sampling artifact?
       GENUINE -- a 10x finer aperture grid produced MORE oscillation
       (6-7 local maxima, up to 8.4x peak-to-peak swings), the opposite
       of what a sampling artifact would do. Confirms and extends
       stage3_human_vision's original finding.
  STATUS: adopted as the standing cross-check/reference dataset; does not
  replace stage3a or stage3_human_vision, both of which remain in use for
  their own original purposes.
