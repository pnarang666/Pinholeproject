- (undated, separate analysis pass, see model_comparison/ at repo root) --
  this project's OWN deprecated closed-form RSS formula was audited
  against this stage's exact numerical pipeline: diverges by 64.8% mean
  (192.8% max) at the retina plane. Separately, and more significantly:
  using r_EE(50%) here to predict real clinical acuity (Wang 2009 dataset)
  overestimates blur by ~7x; FWHM (core-width, not cumulative-energy)
  tracks it to ~1.6x instead.
  STATUS: this is a finding ABOUT which of this folder's own metrics to
  trust for clinical-acuity prediction (r_EE(50%) downgraded in favor of
  FWHM for that specific purpose) -- not an abandonment of the folder or
  its pipeline as a whole.
